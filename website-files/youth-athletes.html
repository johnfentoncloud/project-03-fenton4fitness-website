Who it is for

Development at the right pace

Fenton4Fitness works with youth athletes who want to move better, build a useful strength foundation, and approach sport with greater confidence. Training is adapted to the athlete’s age, experience, current ability, and sport demands.

Exact age ranges and current group availability are confirmed during the inquiry process.

What we develop

  • Movement quality and body control
  • Age-appropriate strength and power
  • Speed, agility, and coordination
  • Confidence and positive training habits
  • Durability and preparation for sport
  • Communication, effort, and accountability

Ways to train

A format for the athlete and the goal

Small groups

Focused coaching, shared energy, and appropriate individual adjustments within a structured group.

Individual training

One athlete, focused attention, and programming shaped around development needs and goals.

Team training

Organized development for teams, built around group needs, sport demands, and the team schedule.

Inside a session

Purpose in every part

01

Prepare

Dynamic movement, coordination, and readiness for the work ahead.

02

Learn

Clear instruction and practice of movement or strength technique.

03

Apply

Speed, strength, power, or conditioning work appropriate to the athlete.

04

Review

Feedback, questions, and a clear understanding of what went well.

Session content varies by athlete, group, season, and program. Safety and quality take priority over chasing arbitrary numbers.

Parent FAQs

Questions are welcome

Does my athlete need strength-training experience?

No. Coaching begins at the athlete’s current level. Technique and appropriate progression come first.

Will both John and Jess coach every session?

Not necessarily. Training may be led by John, Jess, or both depending on the program, scheduling, expertise, and client fit.

Is this only for lacrosse players?

No. John and Jess have girls lacrosse coaching experience, but athletic development principles can support athletes from a variety of sports.

How do we learn the schedule, location, and cost?

Submit an inquiry. A coach will follow up to discuss the athlete, current availability, training options, and pricing without pressure.
