Adult personal training

Practical coaching. Real-life progress.

Individualized strength and fitness coaching for adults who want to feel more capable, learn good technique, and build consistency—at any starting point.

Tell Us About Your Goals

You do not need to be an athlete

Training that meets you where you are

Whether you are new to training, returning after time away, or already experienced, coaching is shaped around your goals, experience, and life outside the gym.

Build strength

Develop useful strength with clear instruction and progressions you understand.

Move with confidence

Practice proper technique and become more comfortable in the gym and daily life.

Create consistency

Build a realistic training rhythm that supports body composition, energy, and an active lifestyle.

What coaching looks like

Personal, clear, and adaptable

We begin by understanding your goals, training history, schedule, and concerns. From there, the work is adjusted to your experience and progress. Sessions may be led by John or Jess based on client fit, program needs, expertise, and availability.

No exaggerated promises. No expectation that every adult wants to train like a competitive athlete. Just thoughtful coaching and honest progress.

Common goals

  • General strength and fitness
  • Body-composition support
  • Confidence using gym equipment
  • Technique and movement quality
  • Returning to a regular routine
  • Training for an active lifestyle
