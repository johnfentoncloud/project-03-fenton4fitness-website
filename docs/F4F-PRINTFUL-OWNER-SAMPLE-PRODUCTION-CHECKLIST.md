  approving a genuinely large back print
- [ ] Record final Design Maker width: ______ in
- [ ] Record final Design Maker height: ______ in
- [ ] Record final Design Maker DPI/quality status: ______
- [ ] Confirm all text is readable and the design is proportioned correctly
- [ ] Confirm no transparent-canvas margin causes the visible art to appear
  smaller or lower than intended

The current back file contains only 732 native pixels across its visible
artwork. Its maximum visible width at Printful's recommended minimum 150 DPI is
approximately 4.88 in. A 9 in back design would be about 81 effective DPI; a
10 in design would be about 73 effective DPI. That is below the approved
quality target.

For a 9–10 in wide back print, request at least:

- Minimum: 1500 px visible-art width for 10 in at 150 DPI
- Preferred: 2700–3000 px visible-art width for 9–10 in at 300 DPI
- Best source: vector artwork exported to a transparent PNG at the exact
  Printful placement dimensions

Do not create a routine resample merely by increasing pixel dimensions or
changing the PNG's DPI metadata. That does not create missing detail. If
Printful offers an automatic image enhancement, compare it closely against the
approved original because automated enhancement can change edges and text.
