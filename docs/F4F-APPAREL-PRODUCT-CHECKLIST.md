## Sample acceptance checks

Record the final Design Maker/Product Creator quote, provider, variant
availability, printable-area dimensions, artwork DPI warnings, and delivery
estimate before checkout. After receipt:

1. Measure chest width, length, sleeve, and artwork placement.
2. Check collar recovery, seam quality, opacity, hand feel, breathability, and
   movement during coaching, lifting, and conditioning.
3. Inspect both prints for sharpness, adhesion, color accuracy, edge lifting,
   excessive film feel, and show-through.
4. Photograph the unworn shirt and repeat inspection after at least five
   care-label-compliant wash cycles.
5. Approve public claims, sizing, pricing, and provider only after the wear test.
